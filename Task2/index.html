Phishing Awareness Program

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers try to trick users into sharing sensitive information or clicking unsafe links.

How to Identify Phishing

  • Check the sender's email address.
  • Be careful with unexpected links or attachments.
  • Check website URLs carefully.
  • Do not share passwords or sensitive information.

Common Social Engineering Techniques

  • Urgent messages
  • Fake offers or rewards
  • Impersonation
  • Fear or pressure tactics

Security Tips

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Verify suspicious messages before responding.
  • Keep your software updated.

Phishing Case Study

Example: A user receives an email saying that their account will be closed unless they verify their information immediately.

The message contains an unexpected link and creates urgency. This can be a warning sign of phishing.

What should you do?
  • Do not click the suspicious link.
  • Check the sender's address carefully.
  • Verify the message using the organization's official website.
  • Report the suspicious message if possible.
